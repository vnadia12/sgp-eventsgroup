About SGP

An event organizer built around one idea - get every detail right.

SGP Events Group is a Singapore-based event organizer and management company. We help clients plan, build, and run events from the first concept sketch to the last guest leaving the room.

We work across two worlds at once: structured corporate programs that need precision, and personal celebrations - gala dinners, wedding anniversaries, milestone birthdays — that need warmth and a distinct visual signature. Both get the same standard of planning, delivered in our signature black, white, and gold.

Indonesia MRO Summit 2026

LocationRadisson Golf & Convention Center Batam, Indonesia
Date9 June 2026 – 10 June 2026
Guests0
FormatMRO Summit

About the event

Indonesia MRO Summit (IMROS) 2026 is a conference and exhibition bringing together key stakeholders from the aviation Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul (MRO) industry to discuss market developments, opportunities, technology, regulations, investment, infrastructure, sustainability, and workforce development. Under the theme "Indonesia Path to Becoming Asia MRO Hub," IMROS provides a platform for airlines, MRO providers, OEMs, regulators, investors, and technology companies to exchange insights, build strategic partnerships, and strengthen Indonesia's position as a regional MRO hub.
